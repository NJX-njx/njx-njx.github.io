## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I get an update when a new post comes out?**

A: You can subscribe to the [RSS feed](/index.xml) to get notified. I also occasionally post updates on [Twitter/X](https://x.com/XS6393865633072).

---

**Q: What tools do you use for this blog?**

A: This blog is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) using the [PaperMod](https://github.com/adityatelange/hugo-PaperMod) theme, and hosted on GitHub Pages.

---

**Q: What if I find something incorrect in a post?**

A: Feel free to open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/NJX-njx/njx-njx.github.io/issues) or reach out to me directly. I appreciate all feedback and corrections! 🙏

---

**Q: Can I translate your posts to another language?**

A: Yes, please go ahead! Just keep a link to the original post at the top. I'd love to know if you do — feel free to let me know!

---

**Q: Do you update your old posts?**

A: Yes, I update posts periodically when I learn something new or find errors. Updated posts will have a note at the top indicating when they were last modified.

---

**Q: What topics do you write about?**

A: I mainly document my learning journey in technology. Topics may include programming, software development, AI/ML, and other tech-related subjects that I'm exploring.
