## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Njx'Log?**

A: A public notebook for the things I am building and thinking through: AI products, evals, agentic RL, multimodal systems, and the engineering details that usually hide behind demos.

---

**Q: What kind of posts will appear here?**

A: Mostly field notes. Some are build logs, some are experiment reports, some are product reflections. I prefer concrete traces: what changed, what broke, what the metric said, and what I still do not know.

---

**Q: What will you not write here?**

A: I try not to write generic AI commentary. If a post has no code, no product scar, no eval detail, or no honest uncertainty, it probably belongs somewhere else.

---

**Q: Can I cite, translate, or share a post?**

A: Yes. Keep a link to the original post, and feel free to tell me where it traveled. I like seeing notes find another desk.

---

**Q: Are you open to collaboration or conversation?**

A: Yes, especially around AI products, eval infrastructure, agent workflows, multimodal applications, and strange little tools that make research easier to use.

---

**Q: How can I reach you?**

A: The cleanest paths are [GitHub](https://github.com/NJX-njx), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/NJX-njx), or [Twitter / X](https://x.com/NJX_njx_ai).
