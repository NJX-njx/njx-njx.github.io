## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Njx'Log?**

A: A public notebook on the engineering side of modern LLM training: harness and rollout systems, model architecture, mid-training and post-training, evals and benchmarks, reasoning RL and agentic RL, RSI, data synthesis — the details that usually hide inside framework defaults.

---

**Q: What kind of posts will appear here?**

A: Mostly field notes and mechanism teardowns: what a knob actually does, what breaks when you turn it, and what the metric said. Longer posts often come with an interactive report edition where the charts are explorable.

---

**Q: What are the interactive reports?**

A: The Reports section turns longer posts into standalone interactive pages — formulas become sliders, curves become probes. Each report is linked from its matching blog post.

---

**Q: What will you not write here?**

A: I try not to write generic AI commentary. If a post has no code, no experiment trace, no eval detail, or no honest uncertainty, it probably belongs somewhere else.

---

**Q: Can I cite, translate, or share a post?**

A: Yes. Keep a link to the original post, and feel free to tell me where it traveled. I like seeing notes find another desk.

---

**Q: Are you open to collaboration or conversation?**

A: Yes, especially around RL infrastructure (rollout and serving), post-training recipes, eval design, agentic systems, and strange little tools that make research easier to use.

---

**Q: How can I reach you?**

A: The cleanest paths are [GitHub](https://github.com/NJX-njx), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/NJX-njx), or [X](https://x.com/NJX_njx_ai).
